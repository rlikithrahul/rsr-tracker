Privacy Policy — RSR Tracker

Last updated: 13 November 2026

RSR Tracker is a private business application used only by RSR Constructions, Visakhapatnam, India. It is not offered to the public and has no public sign-up.

What the app does with Google

The app's backup service connects to one Google account owned by the business owner. It uses that connection only to (1) save encrypted-in-transit backup files of the business's own records into a single Google Drive folder named "RSR Tracker Backups" that it created, and (2) send automatic emails (backup failure alerts and password-reset codes) to the owner's own address.

What we do not do

We do not read, list or change any other Drive files or any other email. We do not sell, share, advertise with or transfer Google data to anyone. Backup files contain the business's own records and are visible only to the account owner.

Retention and deletion

Daily backups are kept for 30 days, plus the first-of-month backup for 12 months, then deleted automatically. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or delete the backup folder.
